I got thousands in cloud credits without a VC. Here's exactly how — and where most founders miss out.

---

## Microsoft for Startups

**Microsoft for Startups (Founders Hub)** is one of the most generous programs available, and it's underused. You get up to $150,000 in Azure credits, GitHub Enterprise, and access to OpenAI models. The application is straightforward — describe what you're building and apply directly at foundershub.startups.microsoft.com.

I applied for MagicSell and got approved. The Azure credits are more than enough for a bootstrapped founder to run infra for months.

## Google for Startups

**Google for Startups** gives access to Google Cloud credits, technical support, and mentorship. Apply directly through their program. They're selective but if you have any traction or a clear use case, it's worth the effort.

The application asks about your use case — be specific. Don't say "we build software." Say "we run serverless functions on X at Y requests per day."

## Cloudflare for Startups

Apply directly through Cloudflare's startup program at cloudflare.com/forstartups. You get Workers credits, pro plan access, and a few months of their business features.

I got this for MagicSell by applying directly — no third-party needed.

## PostHog for Startups

**PostHog** gives $50,000 in credits through their startup program. If you're using PostHog for analytics and feature flags (which you should be), apply for this before paying anything.

## Third-party credit aggregators — worth knowing about

Services like **Spendbase** and **F6S** aggregate startup deals and can get you credits across multiple platforms from one application. Worth signing up — they list deals you might not find otherwise.

## The application that works

Most of these forms ask some version of: "What are you building and why does it need infrastructure?"

What doesn't work: "We're building an AI startup that will disrupt X."

What works: "We're processing [N] Shopify webhooks per month. We use Workers for edge processing and R2 for asset storage. We expect to grow to [Y] by Q3. The credits will let us scale without burning cash on infra before we hit revenue."

Be specific. Give numbers. Show you understand your own stack.

## What I got for MagicSell

- Microsoft for Startups: Azure credits
- Google for Startups: Google Cloud credits
- PostHog: $50,000 in credits
- Cloudflare for Startups: Workers credits (applied directly)

For a bootstrapped solo founder, that covers months of infra without any investment.

---

**The takeaway**

Apply for Microsoft for Startups first — it's the most accessible and most valuable. Add Google for Startups and PostHog. Apply to Cloudflare directly. Most founders leave thousands on the table by not applying.

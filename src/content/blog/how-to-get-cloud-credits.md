## AWS Activate vs GCP for Startups

Both programs give you free cloud credits. The amounts differ and so do the requirements.

**AWS Activate** has two tiers. The Founders tier gives $1,000 in credits and is basically open to anyone with a startup idea. The Portfolio tier goes up to $100,000 but requires a backing organization (accelerator, VC, or incubator). Apply for Founders immediately — no gatekeeping.

**GCP for Startups** gives up to $200,000 over two years depending on your stage. They're more selective but if you have any traction or are in an accelerator, it's worth applying. The application asks about your use case — be specific. Don't say "we build software." Say "we run serverless functions on X at Y requests per day."

## The YC Deal Portal — open to everyone

People don't know this. YC's deal portal (startups.ycombinator.com) is not just for YC companies. Anyone can sign up. It gives you deals on AWS, Stripe, Notion, Linear, Brex, and dozens more.

I signed up. Got access to deals worth more than the cost of applying, which was free. Do this first.

## Stripe Atlas, Vercel, Cloudflare startup programs

**Stripe Atlas** ($500 to set up your company) comes with $5,000 in AWS credits and Stripe fee waivers. Worth it if you're incorporating anyway.

**Vercel for Startups** — apply through their program. They give free Pro plans for early-stage companies. Same for **Netlify**.

**Cloudflare for Startups** — $250 in Workers credits and a few months of their Pro plan. Small but free.

## The application that works

Most of these forms ask some version of: "What are you building and why does it need infrastructure?"

What doesn't work: "We're building an AI startup that will disrupt X."

What works: "We're processing [N] Shopify webhooks per month. We use Workers for edge processing and R2 for asset storage. We expect to grow to [Y] by Q3. The credits will let us scale without burning cash on infra before we hit revenue."

Be specific. Give numbers. Show you understand your own stack.

## What I got for MagicSell

- AWS Founders tier: $1,000
- YC Deals: various (Stripe waivers, Notion credits)
- Cloudflare for Startups: $250 Workers credits

Total: roughly $2,000+ in infrastructure value without any investment. For a bootstrapped solo founder, that covers months of infra.

Apply for AWS Activate today — it takes 20 minutes. Sign up for the YC deal portal next. Defer GCP until you have traction. Most founders leave thousands on the table by not applying.
